three-term contingency

see: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-term_contingency

  1. Antecedent stimulus (a signal that the reinforcement/punishment is available)
  2. Behaviour (the behaviour that is conditioned)
  3. Reinforcement/behaviour

This becomes momentous/significant when combined with the doctrine: “all instrumental/operant behaviour including verbal behaviour, both that of the speaker and that of listener, is acquired, maintained and abandoned in accordance with the principle of the three term contingency (Skinner 1969)” from local:place1998-behaviourismAsAStandpointInLinguistics.pdf

The inadequate complexity of all three elements is where we can invoke much the same argument as for wn.notes:confirmation_holism

non-operant behaviour is: reflexive (automatically elicited by sitmulus) classically conditioned (Pavlovian) not dependent upon reinforcement elicited rather than emitted most importantly – controlled by antecedent stimuli not by consequences
