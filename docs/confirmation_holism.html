thesis

RLN Confirmation is a relationship between at least two entities. On one side of this relationship is the entity that is confirmed, and on the other is the entity that confirms it. Whatever it is that is confirmed might only be confirmed to some degree, in which case confirmation is a relationship between three entities – the thing confirmed, the thing confirming it, and the degree to which it is confirmed (perhaps this last entity is a real number or some other quantity). There may be further entities, such that the entities that are confirmed are only confirmed relative to those further entities. Oft suggested candidates for these further entities include background hypotheses, prior probabilities and theories. Confirmation holism, at least in the sense intended in this work, does not carry any commitment to the nature of existence of any of these further entities.

In any case confirmation is a relationship between (at least) entities that are confirmed and the entities that confirm them.

The things that are confirmed are representations, things that can be true or false,
