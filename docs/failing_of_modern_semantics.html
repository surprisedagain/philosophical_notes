We consider a situation in which a sentence might have different meanings. In this condition or in this context this sentence is true (or better yet grammatical) but in this context the setnece is clearly false (or better yet ungrammatical)

One, purely lingusitic change of context is exemplified by the first example from the article: https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/dynamic-semantics/

Mary met a student yesterday. He needed help.

In this (somehow annoyingly trivial) example the meaning of He needed help. depends upon its context which includes the sentence preceeding it. Another only half remembered example was He passed the ball forwards or something similar - was it the different meanings of passed, and the specific standard in the context of rugby - or was it aome jargon term of soccer which changes meaning in context.
