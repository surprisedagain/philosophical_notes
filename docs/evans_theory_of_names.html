Gödel-Schmidt

Suppose that the “only thing we know about Godel” is that he is the author of the Gödel's theorem. But suppose also that another German in fact wrote that theorem but Gödel somehow successfully stole the credit. We all believe that Gödel wrote the theorem and the only description most of us can provide

This is really a specific version of a more general problem. If we know the meaning of “Gödel” or “Superman” or “Scott” or of any of the names of external objects whose existence we can doubt, or be mistaken about – the objects we only know and can refer to “by description” – then why can't we all provide the definite descriptions on request?
