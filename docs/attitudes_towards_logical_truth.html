Traditional options include:

  • fundamental metaphysical structure grasped by the reasoning mind.
  • analytic truths made true by the meaning of the terms “or” “and”

My view is that they are contingent but self evident truths in the sense that in each world they are believed/considered/uttered they are true.

In the worlds in which they are not uttered/considered/ Not true in the sense that their negation is true, but in the sense that they do not feature in true theories of those simple worlds.
